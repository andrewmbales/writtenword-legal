Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my data across devices?

If you're signed in to iCloud on your iPad, your highlights, bookmarks, notes, and annotations sync automatically. No account or setup is required beyond iCloud sign-in.

Is my data private?

Yes. All study data is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud account. We never see your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which Bible translation is included?

WrittenWord includes the New American Standard Bible, 1995 edition (NASB 1995), licensed from The Lockman Foundation.

Do I need Apple Pencil?

Apple Pencil is supported for handwritten annotations but is not required. You can use highlights, bookmarks, typed notes, and all other study features without one.
